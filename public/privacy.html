Privacy & Safety

This app works two ways, and they handle data differently. By default everything stays on your device. If you choose to sign in, your roster is stored on our server so co-coaches can share it. This page explains both.

The short version: Don't sign in, and nothing about your team leaves your device. Sign in, and your roster, saved games and Google account details are stored on our server and shared with coaches you invite.

Using the app without signing in

This is the default. No account, no server storage.

  • Your data is kept in your browser's local storage — the roster, saved games, and your settings.
  • Nothing about your team is sent to our server. The only network requests are the ones that load the app itself.
  • It works offline. Once loaded, the app runs with no connection.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers. The app loads no third-party scripts at all.
Correcting an earlier version of this page: local data is not erased when you close the tab. It stays in your browser until you clear it, so a shared or family computer will still have your roster on it next time. Use Clear All in the app to remove it.

If you sign in for cloud sync

Signing in with Google turns on syncing, so more than one coach can work from the same roster. That means data is stored on our server.

What is stored on the server

DataWhat it includes
Your account The email address, display name and profile picture URL from your Google account
Your roster Each player's name, jersey number, captain / must-rest / no-keeper flags, availability status and preferred positions
Saved games Game name, date, your notes, the lineup, the settings used, and a snapshot of the roster at the time
Teams and access Team names, age divisions, who is a member, their role, and pending invitations
Your session A sign-in cookie, so you stay signed in between visits

Who can see it

  • You, and anyone you invite to your team.
  • Coaches you invite can view and edit the roster and games.
  • Viewers you invite can only look.
  • Invitation links expire — seven days by default, thirty at most.
  • Team members only ever reach teams they belong to; every request is checked against your membership and role.

Third parties involved

  • Google — only to sign you in. We receive your email, name and profile picture URL; we never see your password.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the server and its database.

There are no advertising networks, analytics services or tracking pixels, signed in or not.

Children's information

This app exists to organise youth soccer teams, so it necessarily holds children's names. We keep that to a minimum: a name, a jersey number, and playing preferences. There are no photos, no birthdates, no addresses, no contact details, and no way for players or families to log in or message anyone.

  • Use first name and last initial where you can — enough to run a lineup, less to expose.
  • Don't put anything else in the name field, such as a phone number or a medical note.
  • Remember that signing in stores those names on our server, not just your device. If you would rather they didn't leave your device, don't sign in — the app is fully functional without it.
  • Check with your AYSO region if you're unsure what their policy allows you to store or share.

Removing your data

  • On your device: use Clear All on the roster tab, and Clear All History on the season tracker.
  • On the server: deleting a team removes its roster, its saved games and its membership records along with it.
  • Exported files — text, CSV and PDF — are saved to your own device and are yours to manage.

How the app is protected

  • The browser is instructed to load scripts only from this site, so no third-party code can run on the page.
  • State-changing requests carry a CSRF token, so another site cannot act on your behalf.
  • The sign-in cookie cannot be read by JavaScript, and is sent over HTTPS in production.
  • API requests are rate limited.
  • Every team request is authorised against your membership and role before it runs.
  • Dependencies are monitored for known vulnerabilities, and the test suite runs on every change.

Questions

This tool is provided free for the AYSO community. For questions about what your region permits, speak to your AYSO region's board. For questions about the app itself, the source code is public and can be inspected.
